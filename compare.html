What this means

  • You own the P.O. Box job

    No app or freight company is set up to collect someone's mail from a post office box. That's yours, and the site should lead with it.

  • The gap is booking, not service

    Uber takes a request at midnight. You can't yet. A form and chat close that gap without changing how you run the truck.

  • Mountain mail problems are real

    Local press covers packages bounced back down the mountain, and wildfire closures in 2024 sent residents to other post offices. Those are your best selling points.

What goes live after you approve

The demo shows the experience. These are the parts that make it work for real.

  1. Requests reach you instantly. The form and chat send each request to your phone, and the customer gets an automatic text confirming it was received.
  2. The chat learns your answers. It's trained on your real hours, towns, and policies, and hands off anything it can't answer.
  3. Search clean-up. Written Google description, fixed social links, and a Google Business Profile so you show up on Maps.
  4. Business email at your own domain, alongside or replacing the Yahoo address.
  5. A few things only you can confirm: the towns you cover, your start year, how you'd like to quote prices, and whether you want reviews collected after each run.

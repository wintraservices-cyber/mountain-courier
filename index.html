What we handle

Skip the trip to the post office, the line at the counter, and the drive in the snow.

  • Mail and package pickup

    We collect from your P.O. Box or the post office counter and bring it to your home or business.

  • Outgoing packages

    We pick up labeled packages from your door and drop them off with the carrier.

  • Banking runs

    Deposits and change orders for local businesses, on a schedule that fits your week.

  • Business mail

    Regular mail handling for offices, shops, and rentals, so nothing sits in a box for days.

  • Seasonal residents

    Away for the winter or only up on weekends? Ask how we can hold, deliver, or forward your mail.

  • Packages that come back

    Carriers often reroute mountain deliveries to the post office. We go get them before they're sent back.

How it works

Three steps, and you never have to leave the house.

  1. Send a request

    Use the form or the chat, day or night. Tell us where to pick up and where it's going.

  2. Get a confirmation

    We text or email you with the run time and the price before anything is scheduled.

  3. We deliver

    Your mail or package arrives on the next available run, Monday to Friday.

Questions people ask

Which towns do you cover?

Lake Arrowhead and the surrounding mountain communities. Send your address with your request and we'll confirm it's on the route.

How much does a pickup cost?

It depends on the run and how often you need it. You'll get the exact price by text or email before anything is booked.

Can you pick up from my P.O. Box?

Yes. We'll walk you through the authorization the post office needs so we can collect on your behalf.

My package says delivered but it went to the post office. Can you help?

Yes, that happens often on the mountain. Send us the tracking number and we'll pick it up before it's returned to the sender.

Do you run on weekends or in snow?

Runs go out Monday to Friday. In heavy weather we'll text you if a run needs to move to the next safe day.
